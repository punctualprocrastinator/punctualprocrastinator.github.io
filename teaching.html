I write and teach Redarc Fellows, the eight-session mechanistic interpretability cohort at Redarc Labs. There is no interpretability library in the stack: hooks are written by hand, because people learn the thing faster when nothing is hidden from them.

One rule is set in week one and repeated every week after it: a rigorous investigation with a careful negative result counts as a success. Two papers came out of the first cohort.

Curriculum

Session 1
90 min

Why did it say that?

Open a language model, trace how it builds an answer layer by layer, and compare Hindi and French.

hooks, logit lens, residual stream
Milestone: Teams formed, with two or three candidate concepts.

Session 2
90 min

Make it say something else

Change the model’s output by adding a vector to its residual stream, then measure the cost.

steering, contrastive pairs, working range
Milestone: Ten to twenty contrastive pairs and one steering demo.

Session 3
90 min

Prove it

Turn the previous week’s result into a measurement with a versioned eval set, a baseline, and controls.

evaluation, controls, model-written evals
Milestone: Versioned eval set, baseline number, repository.

Session 4
90 min

Where does it actually happen?

Use activation patching to locate where the output depends on a given site.

activation patching, causal evidence, attribution patching
Milestone: Patching heatmap for your concept, and a draft started.

Session 5
90 min

What can you read off a vector?

Train a probe on activations and learn why a high accuracy alone proves little.

probes, control tasks, representation geometry
Milestone: Probe with a control-task baseline.

Session 6
120 min

Attack the figure

Teams present their best figure for group critique, then rebuild it to a reviewer-acceptable spec.

figures, triangulation, seeds
Milestone: The figure that becomes Figure 1.

Session 7
90 min

Make it survive review

Turn the figure into a paper.

writing, peer review, methods
Milestone: Introduction and methods draft, plus a review of another team’s draft.

Session 8
120 min

Why is your work cool?

Teams give talks.

demo day, talks, submission
Milestone: Talk delivered, with a venue and date set for the write-up.
