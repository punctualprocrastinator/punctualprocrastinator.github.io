When Does Synthetic Relational Data Teach Models to Use Relations? Tracing Predictive Structure from Pretraining Data to Model Behavior

NeurIPS 2026 · ATTRIB Workshop

Shivam Dubey, Mohamed Bouadi, Nassim Bouarour, Varun Kulkarni, Aditya Tanna, Vinay Kumar Sankarapu

Synthetic relational pretraining data is supposed to teach a tabular foundation model to use relations. This traces the predictive structure from the pretraining distribution through to model behaviour, and separates the cases where the model genuinely learns to use relations from the cases where it only appears to.

Mohamed Bouadi, Nassim Bouarour, Shivam Dubey, Aditya Tanna, Vinay Kumar Sankarapu

Attributing what a tabular foundation model does at inference back to the synthetic data it was pretrained on — behaviour to provenance, rather than the usual direction.

Context Poisoning in Tabular ICL: Geometric Hijacking and Inverse Scaling

2026 — mechanistic interpretability

Shivam Dubey

The first logit-lens implementation for tabular in-context learning: a classification phase transition at Layer 8, representation hijacking as early as Layer 4, and total hijacking from 1.2% poisoned context. Larger models are more vulnerable, not less. QK/OV decomposition and direct logit attribution show that mechanistically-informed defences — PCA subspace scrubbing, head ablation — fail because of manifold redundancy.
