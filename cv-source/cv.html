Research Focus

AI safety researcher working on mechanistic interpretability and adversarial robustness. Current focus is white-box monitoring for AI control: building activation-level monitors, then attacking them with adaptive attackers. Core methods are QK/OV circuit decomposition, direct logit attribution, activation patching, steering vectors, SAE feature attribution and spectral dynamics. Now extending this to non-model capability gains: what control and interpretability look like for the harness a model runs in, not only the model. The approach throughout is understand, exploit, then defend.
