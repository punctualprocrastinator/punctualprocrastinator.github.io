Capability research with the Tabular Foundation Models Lab at Lexsi Labs. The question underneath all of it is provenance: which parts of a synthetic pretraining distribution actually produce a capability, and how you would know from the model's behaviour which part was responsible.

Published and accepted

When Does Synthetic Relational Data Teach Models to Use Relations? Tracing Predictive Structure from Pretraining Data to Model Behavior

NeurIPS 2026 · ATTRIB Workshop — first author

Shivam Dubey, Mohamed Bouadi, Nassim Bouarour, Varun Kulkarni, Aditya Tanna, Vinay Kumar Sankarapu

Synthetic relational pretraining data is supposed to teach a tabular foundation model to use relations. This traces the predictive structure from the pretraining distribution through to model behaviour, and separates the cases where the model genuinely learns to use relations from the cases where it only appears to.

From Behavior to Provenance: Attributing Tabular Foundation Models to Synthetic Pretraining Data

NeurIPS 2026 · ATTRIB Workshop

Mohamed Bouadi, Nassim Bouarour, Shivam Dubey, Aditya Tanna, Vinay Kumar Sankarapu

Attributing what a tabular foundation model does at inference back to the synthetic data it was pretrained on — behaviour to provenance, rather than the usual direction.

What You Pretrain On Matters: Synthetic Task Distributions Determine Tabular Foundation Model Quality

ICML 2026 · FMSD Workshop — accepted poster

Mohamed Bouadi, Nassim Bouarour, Varun Kulkarni, Aditya Tanna, Shivam Dubey, Vinay Kumar Sankarapu

Introduces O'Prior, a compositional realism prior for synthetic pretraining of tabular foundation models. I ran the interpretability and data-quality ablations, which confirm that mechanism diversity, realism composition, and shift-aware stress each contribute independently to downstream quality.

Physics-Informed Neural Operators with Multi-Method Explainability for Ocean Temperature Prediction

AAAI 2026 · XAI4Science Workshop — sole author

Shivam Dubey

Neural operators for ocean temperature prediction, with the physics constraint and the explanation methods carrying equal weight: a forecast you cannot interrogate is not much use to the people who have to act on it.
