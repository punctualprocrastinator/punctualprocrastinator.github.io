Why interpretability findings need a calibrated null

Naive significance testing flags up to 100% of findings on untrained models. A K ≥ 8–12 null distribution corrects the false-positive rate. This is the argument behind dead-salmon-fpr, written out properly for people who have not read the code.

What a negative result rules out, and what it does not

At MARS I found that jailbreak success and probe evasion were mutually exclusive across three attack families. That is a real constraint on a narrow class of attacks, and it is not a safety guarantee. The gap between those two readings is where most of the interesting disagreement sits.
