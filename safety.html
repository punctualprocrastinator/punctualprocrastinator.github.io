Toxin Feature Hierarchy in ESM-2: Why Frozen Probes Resist ProteinMPNN Redesign

GenBio 2026 · Poster

Shivam Dubey, Manan Wadhwa

BLAST detects 0% of 643 ProteinMPNN redesigns; a frozen ESM-2 linear probe detects 93.9% with no retraining. SAE analysis shows why: redesign amplifies toxin structural features rather than obscuring them. A four-tier attack taxonomy places the security boundary at gradient access — 6.1% evasion black-box against 100% white-box — and direct probe attribution pinpoints Layer 32 as the detection bottleneck (r = 0.992).

White-box monitoring under adaptive attack · MARS V

Linear compliance probes trained on residual-stream activations across four instruction-tuned models from three labs (Qwen2.5-7B, Llama-3.1-8B, Gemma-2-9B, and an abliterated Llama-3). Compliance is linearly decodable, and the probe tracks refusal itself rather than surface phrasing — length-matched accuracy is indistinguishable across models.

I then ran a red-team/blue-team co-evolution loop against those probes across three attack families: suffix-gradient, discrete search, and activation steering. The headline result is negative. Jailbreak success and probe evasion turned out to be mutually exclusive: the perturbations that evade the monitor also stop the jailbreak working.

The extension into trained attack policies — Redwood's open problem on stress-testing white-box monitors — is gated behind a six-stage empirical de-risking protocol. The failure mode I expect to matter most is the system-prompt confound: whether the probe is reading the model's behaviour or merely the instructions it was given. The ablation that separates the two is designed.

Confidence as a separable channel · Redarc Labs

Cohort 01 is built around one thesis: expressed confidence in LLMs is separable from internal knowledge, implemented partly through entropy neurons that modulate output entropy without changing token predictions. I am mentoring three first-author projects on it — attributing hedging to its cause (ignorance, safety training, sycophancy, sandbagging) from internal states; whether confidence regulation is shared across uncertainty types and survives post-training; and whether white-box monitors for that channel can be evaded. Two papers are under review at NeurIPS 2026 workshops.

Isolating Refusal and Compliance Heads: Unconditional Bipolar Defenses

2026 — safety circuits, Qwen2.5-7B

Shivam Dubey

A compliance-score classifier reaches AUC 0.943 (Cohen's d = 2.636) with zero false positives across five benign categories — a working zero-shot white-box harm detector. Unconditional bipolar assertion, amplifying refusal heads while zero-ablating compliance heads at L25-H4, holds attack success to 33% under nanoGCG against 36–42% for conditional gating and 66% undefended: the precision of conditional gating is itself a structural vulnerability under white-box optimisation. Layer 25 shows extreme intra-layer antagonism between the two head types.

Pre-commitment Probing in Open-Weight Reasoning Models

2026 — OLMo-3-7B-Think

Shivam Dubey

A linear probe on the residual stream predicts answer correctness before any reasoning token is emitted (AUC 0.780, 5-fold CV) — the model commits before it reasons, which makes the chain of thought post-hoc rationalisation rather than deliberation. A functional frustration direction at Layer 15 transfers cross-lingually across six languages (p = 0.0017): a sub-linguistic computational state invisible in the output but readable mechanistically, usable either as a monitor or as an attack surface.

Context Poisoning in Tabular ICL: Geometric Hijacking and Inverse Scaling

2026 — tabular foundation models

Shivam Dubey

The first logit-lens implementation for tabular in-context learning: a classification phase transition at Layer 8, representation hijacking as early as Layer 4, and total hijacking from 1.2% poisoned context. Larger models are more vulnerable, not less. QK/OV decomposition and direct logit attribution show that mechanistically-informed defences — PCA subspace scrubbing, head ablation — fail because of manifold redundancy.

Open-source tooling

  • Package neuron_autolabel Auto-interpretability for LLaMA-style models. A hypothesis / test / revise loop per neuron, scored by detection AUC (logit gap) rather than simulation, with held-out splits and shuffled controls.
  • Method dead-salmon-fpr A calibrated null-model test for interpretability claims. Naive significance testing flags up to 100% of findings on untrained models; a K ≥ 8–12 null distribution corrects the false-positive rate.
  • Tool mechanistic-tamper-verification A two-tier integrity verifier, cryptographic plus mechanistic differencing, that detects tampered model updates by the concentration of weight change rather than its magnitude.
